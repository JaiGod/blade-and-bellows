BLADE & BELLOWS

a blacksmith-shop RPG

You've inherited your grandfather's forge in Emberfall and his 2,000-gold debt. Forge weapons by hand, haggle with picky customers, arm adventurers for the dungeons and out-smith your rival in 30 days. An original pixel-art RPG made in Godot 4.

▶ Play in browser
Free · no install · mouse, keyboard or touch · progress saves automatically

Forge it yourself

1 · HeatHold to pump the bellows until the metal glows the right colour for its ore. Too hot and it scorches.
2 · HammerStrike as the marker crosses the sweet spot. Perfect hits throw sparks; the metal cools as you work.
3 · QuenchPlunge the blade into the barrel as its glow fades into the window. Steam, hiss, done.

Features

6 weapons, 5 ores, 6 tiers

Daggers to greatswords in copper, iron, silver, mithril and starmetal, from Crude to Legendary. Four fittings change power and price.

Gem enchanting

Ruby flame, sapphire frost, emerald fortune, topaz haste and amethyst warding.

Haggling customers

Hagglers, nobles, veterans, apprentices, collectors and bards, each with a budget, a wish list and limited patience.

Adventurers & 4 dungeons

Lend your weapons to adventurers. They return with gold, ore, gems, fame and stories, or never return at all.

Upgrades & rivalry

Upgrade the forge, anvil and shop. Survive Vorn's price war, sabotage and two smithing contests.

30-day story

Rent every week, a debt due on day 30 and a champion who needs the finest blade in the land. Four endings.
